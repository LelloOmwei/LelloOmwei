Research Areas

OMWEI Tech provides an open technical space for exploring the foundations of trustworthy autonomy.

Autonomous System Architecture

Exploring architectural principles required for autonomous systems that can operate reliably in complex environments.

  • System boundaries
  • Autonomy models
  • Trust relationships
  • System composition

Execution & Action Governance

Exploring how autonomous decisions become authorized effects in the real world.

  • Execution Authority
  • Authorization mechanisms
  • Commit semantics
  • State transition integrity

Agent Integrity & Identity

Exploring how autonomous entities can be identified, verified, and evaluated.

  • Agent identity
  • Provenance
  • Attestation
  • Runtime integrity

Embodied Autonomy

Exploring trustworthy autonomy in systems interacting with physical environments.

  • Robotics
  • Industrial autonomy
  • Physical AI
  • Safety-critical systems

Assurance & Evaluation

Developing methods to measure, compare, and demonstrate trustworthiness.

  • Evaluation methods
  • Evidence frameworks
  • Verification approaches
  • Assurance models

Technical Foundations

OMWEI Tech focuses on functional properties and measurable guarantees rather than prescribing a single implementation approach.

Functional Requirements

Trustworthy autonomy should be defined by required properties and guarantees, not by a specific technology.

Separation of Intelligence and Authority

The ability to make decisions and the authority to create real-world effects are distinct architectural concerns.

Evidence-Based Engineering

Technical claims should be supported by reproducible experiments and verifiable evidence.
