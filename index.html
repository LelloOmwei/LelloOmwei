Research Areas

OMWEI Tech provides an open technical environment for exploring the foundations required for trustworthy autonomy.

Understanding what an autonomous system is, what it contains, and whether its operational state can be trusted.

  • Agent identity
  • Provenance
  • Attestation
  • Runtime integrity

Execution Governance

Exploring how autonomous decisions become authorized effects in the real world.

  • Execution Authority
  • Authorization boundaries
  • Commit semantics
  • State transition integrity

Embodied Autonomy

Researching trustworthy autonomy in systems interacting with physical environments.

  • Robotics
  • Industrial systems
  • Autonomous vehicles
  • Safety-critical applications

Assurance & Evaluation

Developing methods to evaluate and demonstrate trustworthy autonomous behaviour.

  • Evidence frameworks
  • Verification methods
  • Evaluation methodologies
  • Trust models

Technical Principles

OMWEI Tech focuses on functional properties and measurable guarantees rather than prescribing a single technological approach.

Functional over technological

Trustworthy autonomy should be defined by required properties and guarantees, not by a specific implementation technology.

Evidence over assumptions

Technical claims should be supported by reproducible experiments and verifiable evidence.

Separation of intelligence and authority

The ability to make decisions and the authority to create real-world effects are distinct architectural concerns.
